Welcome to the **User Manual** of Polyphone! To quickly discover what soundfonts are and how to edit them with Polyphone, you can start with [tutorials](tutorials/index.md) or watch videos on <a href="https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=polyphone+sf2" target="_blank">YouTube</a> (special thanks to Polyphone users for their useful videos). And if you'd like to learn all about the software's features, simply read on.

If you need more help, or if reading these pages puts you off, you may find assistance in the [forum](forum).


## Home screen {#doc_home}


The first screen of Polyphone is basically made of 3 parts:

* a [left part](#doc_left), containing a history of open files and directories, as well as shortcuts;
* a [right part](#doc_right), dedicated to the soundfont repository;
* a [top area](#doc_top), containing tabs and a menu.


![Home screen of Polyphone](images/page_home.png "Home screen of Polyphone")


## Left part {#doc_left}


### Opening a soundfont


Several ways for opening a soundfont:

* a click on :guilabel:`Open soundfont` opens a file browser, the selectable files having the extension :file:`.sf2`, :file:`.sf3`, :file:`.sfArk`, :file:`.sfz`, :file:`.dls` or :file:`.organ` (the different formats are described [here](manual/annexes/the-different-soundfont-formats.md)). Several files can be selected at once;
* a click on :guilabel:`New soundfont` opens an empty soundfont, everything having to be done from scratch;
* a click on :guilabel:`Open directory` opens the [soundfont explorer](manual/soundfont-explorer.md), which scans an entire directory and allows you to open the desired file;
* a double-click on a soundfont from the history reopens it (the selected items can also be opened with :kbd:`Enter` and removed from the history with :kbd:`Del`);
* a file or a directory dragged and dropped on the history area, or on the [tab bar](#doc_top) whatever the current tab, is opened.

When a soundfont is open, a new tab appears with a [soundfont editor](manual/soundfont-editor/index.md) inside.


### Shortcuts


Three buttons are located at the bottom of the left part:

* :guilabel:`Documentation`: opens a web browser for reading the Polyphone [documentation](documentation);
* :guilabel:`Forum`: opens a web browser with the URL of the Polyphone [forum](forum);
* :guilabel:`Settings`: opens the [settings](manual/settings.md) of the software in a new tab (also accessible with :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`,`).


## Right part {#doc_right}


### Online repository


A click on the button :guilabel:`Online repository` opens a tab displaying the content of the [online soundfont repository](manual/online-repository.md).


### Daily soundfonts


Every day, 5 random soundfonts are displayed here, to encourage people to discover new soundfonts.
Double-clicking on one of them opens a new tab with the corresponding [soundfont description](manual/online-repository.md#doc_description).
A click on the name of an author or on an attribute of a soundfont opens the [online repository](manual/online-repository.md) with the corresponding filter.


### Search


A keyword can be written here to search for specific soundfonts in the online repository, the results being displayed in the [dedicated tab](manual/online-repository.md).


## Top area {#doc_top}


### Tabs


The tabs provide a quick access between:

* the [home screen](#doc_home) (first tab — always open),
* the [editor](manual/soundfont-editor/index.md) of an open soundfont,
* the [soundfont explorer](manual/soundfont-explorer.md) (one per opened directory),
* the software [settings](manual/settings.md),
* the [online repository](manual/online-repository.md),
* a [soundfont description](manual/online-repository.md#doc_description) from the online repository.

Navigation between tabs can be performed using keyboard shortcuts or the mouse wheel:

* :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`Tab`, :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`PageDown` or :kbd:`Shift`+mouse wheel down over the tab bar to go to the next tab, excluding the home screen tab,
* :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`Shift`+:kbd:`Tab`, :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`PageUp` or :kbd:`Shift`+mouse wheel up over the tab bar to go to the previous tab, excluding the home screen tab,
* :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`Home` and :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`End` to go to the first and the last tab, excluding the home screen tab,
* :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`W` to close the current tab,
* :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`H` to go to the home screen.

The shortcut :kbd:`Ctrl`+:kbd:`F` puts the focus on the search field of the current tab: the search field of the home screen, the search field of the [online repository](manual/online-repository.md), the [search bar of the tree](manual/soundfont-editor/tree.md#doc_search) in a soundfont editor or the search field of the [soundfont explorer](manual/soundfont-explorer.md).
